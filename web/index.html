    Who is a right fit, and why we can defend it

    Sprinto sells compliance automation (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR). The best prospects are B2B software companies, typically Series A to C, that are about to be asked for audit evidence by enterprise customers. The rubric below is the whole policy: it is loaded live from the same file the pipeline hashes.

    How a score becomes a verdict

    Every signal is either known (points from a quoted fact) or unknown. The company gets a minimum score (unknown counts 0), a maximum (unknown counts full weight) and an expected score: the minimum plus 0.5 of the gap to the maximum, so each unresolved signal counts for that share of its weight.

    • Qualified when the minimum is at least 60.
    • Rejected when the maximum is below 45, or a hard gate fails.
    • Otherwise a bounded research loop fetches up to 3 more same-domain pages, each with a logged reason. If the loop has run, at least 2 signals are known from quoted evidence and the expected score is at least 55, the company is qualified on the expected score. That is an estimate and is labelled as one. Anything else is insufficient data.
