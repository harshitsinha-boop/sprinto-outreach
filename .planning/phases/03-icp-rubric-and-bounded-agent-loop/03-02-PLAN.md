## Phase Goal

Every company gets a reproducible, defensible qualification verdict from a written ICP rubric, and insufficient data triggers a capped, logged agent loop instead of a guess.

User-story framing: not available. The ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal is phrased as an outcome, not a story, and MVP rules say not to invent one.

## This plan (tracer first)

This plan fills the gray band that Plan 03-01 leaves as insufficient_data. When the score interval straddles 45..60, a bounded research loop asks Gemini which same-domain page to read next, with a logged reason, and fetches it through the guarded fetcher. It re-extracts the new page with the existing evidence-validated extractor and re-scores in Python after every fetch. It stops on a code-defined stop reason. Task 1 is the tracer: one gray-band company goes through one fetch_page call to a qualified verdict, Hunter and a dry run, across every layer (LLM wrapper, LLMLike seam, fake, agent, qualify, stage, run_one). Task 2 hardens the loop's guards (refusals, caps, stop reasons, no page text to the model, error handling, zero Hunter after an unresolved loop). Task 3 hardens the wrapper (no schema mixing, wire format, verbatim thought signatures, AFC check, caching), so re-running a loop company is free.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 3 success criterion 4, and criterion 5 for post-loop companies (QUA-04, QUA-05).

Output: `src/outreach/agent.py`, `ToolSession` and `build_tool_config` in `src/outreach/llm.py`, the tool and research models, the research seam in `qualify` and `stage_qualify`, a `purpose` argument on `extract_profile`, a tool-session-capable FakeLLM, `tests/test_agent.py`, and new wrapper tests in `tests/test_llm.py`.

The payload is code-generated only: status, url, kind, refusal, resolved_signals, unknown_signals, score_min and score_max.
